Under the legacy manual process, auditors could only randomly sample 10% of tickets, at an average of 45 minutes each (see Legacy Process). At today's volume of 248 tickets processed, that means about 223 tickets a day (90% of volume) received no audit at all. Checking those 223 tickets by hand, at the same 45-minute baseline, would take an estimated 167 hours of manual work per day, more hours than any realistic auditor headcount could cover. This platform scores all 248 tickets automatically instead, so that full coverage happens with zero added manual review time, and auditors only spend time on the 37 tickets flagged as genuine exceptions today.

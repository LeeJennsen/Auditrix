Auto-flag threshold
80
Any ticket scoring below 80, or failing a critical criterion outright, routes to human review instead of auto-clearing.
Critical override
A 0% on Rollback Plan or PIR flags the ticket regardless of overall score; a high combined score can't mask a missing critical control.
This rubric is what makes scoring repeatable across auditors: the weights and threshold are versioned and applied identically to every ticket of that type, so a change here updates how every future ticket of that type is scored, not just how one auditor happens to judge one ticket.
